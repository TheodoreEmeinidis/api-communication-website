      Connection status interpretation

      Using server receipt time: GOOD under 5 seconds; PROBLEM from 5 to under 10 seconds; OFFLINE at 10 seconds or more. A new Connection Ping or recognized activity update refreshes the connection status. This is separate from the activity's running or completion state.
